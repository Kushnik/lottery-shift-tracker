Employee Guide

How to Use This Shift Tracker

  1. Check Shift Info: Confirm the Date, Shift (1, 2, or 3), and your Cashier Name before starting.
  2. Read Front Ticket Numbers: Walk down the counter from Slot #1 to #62. Look at the bottom-right corner of the front ticket in each slot and type the last 2 or 3 digits on the 10-Key Pad, then tap NEXT SLOT →.
  3. Empty or Sold-Out Bins:
    • If a slot was already empty and still has no tickets, tap EMPTY BIN (0 Sold / Skip).
    • If a pack sold completely out down through ticket 000 during your shift, tap PACK SOLD OUT (Through 000).
  4. New Pack Added Mid-Shift: If a pack sells out and you put a new pack in that slot, first mark the old pack PACK SOLD OUT and save/roll, OR enter the starting top ticket number (e.g., 099 or 029) into the OPEN box when loading a fresh pack into an empty slot.
  5. Finish Your Shift: Once all 62 slots are checked, tap Save Shift & Roll Forward. This sends your totals to the Google Sheet and automatically moves your closing numbers into the next shift's opening numbers.
  6. Sold a Ticket After Closing Shift? Tap Recall Last Shift inside the green sales box to bring back your just-closed shift, update the new closing ticket number, and tap Update & Roll Forward. It automatically updates the Google Sheet without double-counting.
